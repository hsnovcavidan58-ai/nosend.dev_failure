Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before choosing a plan.

Are the tools really free?

Yes. The free tier is free forever — no credit card, no account, no email required. You get 10 operations per calendar month and 10 MB per file. Everything runs in your browser.

Do you upload my files?

Never. Every tool runs 100% in your browser. You can verify this yourself — open DevTools (F12), switch to the Network tab, and process a file. You will see zero upload requests.

What is the difference between Premium and Lifetime?

Premium is a recurring subscription ($9/month or $79/year) with all premium features. Lifetime is a one-time payment ($49–$99) that gives you all premium features forever, with no recurring charges.

When can I purchase?

Paid plans become available on 18 January 2027. Until then, every tool is completely free — no limits, no accounts, no payment required.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy, our Merchant of Record. They accept all major credit and debit cards, PayPal, and local payment methods depending on your region.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. All purchases come with a 14-day no-questions-asked refund policy. Contact hello@nosend.dev within 14 days of purchase for a full refund.

Can I cancel my Premium subscription?

Yes, anytime. There are no lock-in contracts. You will retain Premium access until the end of your current billing period, and will not be charged again.

Do you collect any personal data?

Only what is strictly necessary to deliver a paid license: your email address (for license delivery) and payment confirmation from Lemon Squeezy. We never see your card details, and we never collect your files. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
