1. Overview

nosend.dev ("we", "us", "our") operates a collection of file-processing tools that run entirely inside your web browser. Unlike traditional file-conversion and file-editing services, our tools do not require you to upload your files anywhere. Every operation — merging CSV files, converting images, combining PDFs, formatting JSON — is performed on your own device using standard browser APIs such as FileReader, Canvas, WebAssembly, PapaParse, and pdf-lib.

This policy explains what data we do and do not collect, how our tools work, which third-party services are involved, and what rights you have. It applies to nosend.dev and all sub-pages, including individual tool pages.

If you only remember one sentence, remember this one: your files never leave your device, and we have no ability to see, read, or store them.

2. What we do not collect

To be explicit, because this is the entire point of nosend.dev:

  • Your files. Not a single byte of any file you open in any tool is transmitted to any server. There is no upload endpoint, no multipart form, no fetch() request that sends file data.
  • File names, file sizes, or file metadata. We do not log which files you selected, their names, their types, or their sizes.
  • File contents. Everything is processed locally. You can verify this yourself — open DevTools (F12), switch to the Network tab, and process a file. You will see zero upload requests.
  • Your IP address. We do not log IP addresses. Cloudflare, our hosting provider, may retain anonymized edge logs for security and abuse-prevention purposes (see Section 6), but we do not access, export, or analyze those logs.
  • Your email address. No account, no newsletter, no login. We only receive your email if you choose to contact us directly (see Section 14).
  • Tracking cookies or analytics identifiers. No Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, no fingerprinting scripts, no session recording, no heatmaps.
  • Behavioral data. We do not record which tools you use, how often, or from where.

Verifiable claim: open DevTools (F12) → Network tab → process a file. Zero upload traffic. That is the proof — not our word.

3. What we do collect

Very little, but honesty requires listing it. We collect only what is technically unavoidable for a website to function, plus what you voluntarily provide:

3.1. Anonymous page-view counts (via Cloudflare)

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, collects aggregated request counts at the edge (e.g., "this page was served 1,200 times today"). This data is anonymous, aggregated, and used only for capacity planning and abuse prevention. We do not have access to individual visitor records.

3.2. Premium license keys (only if you purchase one)

If you purchase a Premium or Lifetime license (available after 18 January 2027), Lemon Squeezy — acting as Merchant of Record — processes your payment information and issues a license key. We receive the following from Lemon Squeezy: your email address (for license delivery), the license key, and the purchase status. We store only the license key on our side (in Cloudflare Worker secrets), not your payment details.

3.3. Local usage counter (stays on your device)

Free-tier users have a monthly operation limit. To enforce this without a server, we store a numeric counter in your browser's localStorage under the key nosend_usage. This counter never leaves your device. Clearing it simply resets the counter — it does not grant premium access, because premium status is validated separately on the server (see Section 6).

3.4. Emails you send us

If you contact us at hello@nosend.dev or security@nosend.dev, we receive your email address and whatever content you include. We retain these emails only as long as needed to resolve your inquiry.

4. How our tools process your files

Every tool follows the same privacy-first architecture:

  1. You select a file. The file is read directly from your device by the browser's <input type="file"> element. At no point is the file path transmitted anywhere — the browser does not even reveal the full path to JavaScript.
  2. The browser reads the file. Using FileReader or file.arrayBuffer(), the browser loads the file into local memory (RAM) on your device.
  3. Processing happens locally. Depending on the tool, the processing uses:
    • PapaParse (via Web Workers) — for CSV and JSON.
    • pdf-lib — for PDF manipulation (WebAssembly, runs locally).
    • Canvas API — for image compression and conversion.
    • Native JavaScript — for everything else.
  4. The result is created on your device. The output file is generated as a Blob in your browser and offered as a download. It is not uploaded anywhere.
  5. Memory is released. After processing, the tool calls URL.revokeObjectURL() to free the memory used by the temporary object URL. When you close the tab, all remaining memory is released by your browser.

What this means practically: even if we wanted to see your files, we could not. There is no upload endpoint to send them to, no server script that processes them, no database that stores them. The architecture physically prevents data collection.

5. Local storage and cookies

5.1. Cookies

We do not set any cookies. No session cookie, no preference cookie, no tracking cookie, no third-party ad cookie. If this ever changes (for example, if programmatic advertising is introduced post-launch), this policy will be updated before any such cookie is set, and a cookie-consent banner will be implemented where legally required.

5.2. localStorage

We use your browser's localStorage for exactly three small, non-personal values:

Key Purpose Personal data?
nosend_usage Free-tier monthly operation counter No — a single number
nosend_license Your premium license key (only if you purchase one) No — a random key string
nosend_license_cache Cached validation result (1-hour TTL) to avoid repeated server calls No — a boolean + timestamp

You can inspect, modify, or delete all three at any time from DevTools → Application → Local Storage. Deleting them has no security impact on us (nothing to lose), but may reset your usage counter or log you out of premium features until the next validation.

6. Third-party services

A minimum number of trusted vendors are involved in serving the site:

6.1. Cloudflare (hosting and edge)

nosend.dev is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, with a small license-validation endpoint running on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare processes all incoming HTTP requests and may retain anonymized edge logs (IP hashes, request counts, error rates) for security and abuse-prevention. We do not access or export those logs.

Cloudflare's privacy practices: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

6.2. Lemon Squeezy (payment processor — from 18 Jan 2027)

When you purchase a premium license, payment processing is handled by Lemon Squeezy, acting as Merchant of Record. This means Lemon Squeezy is the legal seller of the license, and they collect and process your payment details (card number, billing address, tax information). We never see your card data. We receive only the license key and confirmation of purchase.

Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy: lemonsqueezy.com/privacy.

6.3. jsDelivr (CDN for open-source libraries)

Some tools load open-source JavaScript libraries (PapaParse, pdf-lib) from the jsDelivr CDN. jsDelivr serves these files; it does not receive your file data, because your file data is never in an HTTP request. jsDelivr's access logs record standard CDN metadata (IP, user-agent, requested file), which we do not access.

jsDelivr's privacy policy: jsdelivr.com/privacy-policy.

6.4. No other third parties

No Google Analytics. No Facebook. No advertising networks (at the time of writing). No session recorders. No customer-support chat widgets. No A/B testing platforms. If any of these is added in the future, this policy will be updated first.

7. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) requires us to state a legal basis for any processing of personal data. Our position is simple:

  • We do not process personal data for the tools themselves. All file processing happens on your device. There is no transfer of personal data to us, so no legal basis is required.
  • For the limited data we do handle (email inquiries, license keys), our legal basis is:
    • Contract performance (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) — for license keys and payment confirmation, because we need these to deliver the service you purchased.
    • Legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) — for responding to support emails and for Cloudflare's anonymous abuse-prevention logging.

We do not engage in automated decision-making, profiling, or any processing that would require explicit consent under GDPR.

8. Your rights under GDPR

If GDPR applies to you, you have the following rights. Because we handle almost no personal data, most of these rights are trivially satisfied — there is simply nothing to act on.

Right What it means for nosend.dev
Right of access (Art. 15) You can ask what personal data we hold about you. For most users, the answer is: an email (if you wrote to us) or a license key (if you purchased one). Nothing else.
Right to rectification (Art. 16) Ask us to correct inaccurate data — e.g., a misspelled email address on your license.
Right to erasure (Art. 17) Ask us to delete any personal data we hold. We will do so within 30 days (subject to legal record-keeping obligations for payment records, if any).
Right to restrict processing (Art. 18) Ask us to stop processing while a dispute is resolved.
Right to data portability (Art. 20) Receive your data in a machine-readable format. For a license key plus email, this is a two-line JSON file.
Right to object (Art. 21) Object to processing based on legitimate interest. If you object, we stop unless we have compelling legal grounds.
Right to lodge a complaint (Art. 77) Complain to your local data-protection authority. You do not have to complain to us first.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@nosend.dev with the subject line "GDPR request". We will respond within 30 days.

9. Data retention

We keep data for the shortest time technically necessary:

  • Files: never stored — not a single byte.
  • Support emails: retained until the inquiry is resolved, then deleted within 90 days.
  • License keys: retained as long as the license is active, plus 12 months for accounting and tax purposes.
  • Anonymous edge logs (Cloudflare): retained per Cloudflare's standard policy (typically 24–72 hours for edge logs).
  • Local usage counter: retained in your browser until you clear it. We have no way to read or delete it remotely — it is your data, on your device.

10. Data security

Even though we handle very little data, we take its security seriously:

  • HTTPS everywhere. All traffic to nosend.dev is encrypted with TLS. We do not serve any content over plain HTTP.
  • Strict Content Security Policy. All pages enforce a restrictive CSP via Cloudflare _headers that blocks unauthorized script injection and cross-site exfiltration.
  • Server-side license validation. Your license status is never trusted from the browser — it is validated on every request through our Cloudflare Worker → Lemon Squeezy.
  • No secrets in the frontend. No API keys, no payment secrets, no private tokens are ever exposed in client-side code.
  • Open verification. You can verify our privacy claims in DevTools at any time. If you find a vulnerability, please report it to security@nosend.dev — do not open a public issue.

11. Children's privacy

nosend.dev is a professional tool for developers, lawyers, accountants, and other working adults. It is not directed at children under the age of 16 (or under 13 in jurisdictions where that is the applicable COPPA threshold). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us at hello@nosend.dev and we will delete it promptly.

Note: because the tools themselves collect no personal data whatsoever, children can use the free tools with the same privacy guarantees as anyone else. The concern here is limited to the paid-license flow, which requires a valid payment method.

12. International users

nosend.dev is operated from Azerbaijan. Because we do not transfer personal data as part of providing the tools, the geographic location of the operator is largely irrelevant to your privacy. Where international data transfers do occur (for example, Cloudflare's global edge network), they are governed by standard contractual clauses between us and the vendor, or by the vendor's own GDPR/UK-GDPR compliance framework.

If you are located in a jurisdiction with stricter local data-protection laws than GDPR (for example, certain states in the United States, Brazil, or Japan), your rights under those laws are not diminished by this policy. Contact us at hello@nosend.dev if you have a jurisdiction-specific request.

13. Changes to this policy

  • The "Last updated" date at the top of this page changes.
  • For significant changes (new data collection, new third-party processors), we will display a notice on the homepage for at least 30 days.
  • We will never retroactively change this policy to permit collection of data we previously promised not to collect.

We recommend checking this page occasionally. Continued use of nosend.dev after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the updated terms, but the core promise — your files never leave your browser — is not negotiable and will never change.
