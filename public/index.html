How it works

Three steps. No account. No upload. No data leaves your device.

1. Pick a tool

Choose from CSV, JSON, image, or PDF tools — all free, all browser-based.

2. Drop your file

The file is read directly from your device — never transmitted to a server.

3. Download the result

Your processed file is created in your browser and saved directly to your device.

Why privacy matters

When you upload a document to a traditional tool, it's processed on someone else's server. For legal contracts, financial spreadsheets, or medical records, that's an unacceptable risk.
